Each mutating step in the change plan carries a **mutation manifest**: field, old value, and new value — one field per step, or a coupled set with its `coupling:` rationale — or `created: <resource>` with its source digest for a create; an `ignore:` list of literal field paths its read-back may skip, never a category label; and any `unverifiable: write-only` field with the alternative observable that proves it. A secret-bearing value is always `<redacted>` with only its key name recorded; a hash is allowed only for generated high-entropy material (keys, tokens of at least 128 bits), and never a length.
